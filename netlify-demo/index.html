What arming these headers can break

            
    
              
  • Cross-origin iframes and embeds, such as video players, maps, chat, social and payment widgets, stop loading unless they opt in.
    • 
              
  • Popups that talk back to your page through window.opener, such as OAuth sign-in and payment windows, lose that link.
    • 
              
  • Third-party scripts, images, fonts, ads and analytics fail unless they are served with CORS or Cross-Origin-Resource-Policy: cross-origin.
    • 
            

            
The headers apply to everything the page loads, so test the whole site, not only the widget.

Light mode

            
Each mining thread is its own worker with a private 256 MiB cache, about 300 MB of RAM each.
              It runs on isolated and non-isolated pages alike, slower than full mode per thread.

            

          


          

            
            
            
            
          


          

          

            
            
Gives up to two workers a private copy of the full dataset (about 2.3 GB each), which mines
              roughly 2.25 times as fast as a light worker. The embed works out the best mix of light workers and
              full-dataset ones within the RAM budget on each visitor's device, and uses none where it doesn't pay.
              The workers build the datasets themselves, within the same CPU and RAM limits, before they start
              mining, and again after each seed change (about every three days).

          


          

            
On this device

            

            
Visitors' devices differ: the embed works this out again on each visitor's device, from their
              reported cores and memory.

Snippet

      
      

        

      

        Deployment notes
        
    
          
  • The snippet loads the embed and its engine files from the pinned jsDelivr commit. To self-host, publish the
            whole dist/ directory and serve it with Access-Control-Allow-Origin: * and
            Cross-Origin-Resource-Policy: cross-origin.
    • 
          
  • Light mode downloads worker.js and randomx_st.js/.wasm, plus
            fb_full.js with replicas. Full mode downloads embed-worker.js and
            randomx.js/.wasm. Nothing downloads before consent.
    • 
          
  • With a Content Security Policy, allow the asset origin in script-src and connect-src,
            the bridge in connect-src, blob: workers and WebAssembly:
            
    script-src 'self' https://cdn.jsdelivr.net blob: 'wasm-unsafe-eval';
worker-src 'self' blob:;
connect-src 'self' https://cdn.jsdelivr.net wss://YOUR_BRIDGE;
    • 
          
  • The bridge sees your site as the WebSocket Origin; allow it there. Match the pool shown to
            visitors to the pool the bridge actually connects to.
    •
